Privacy Policy

Last Updated: February 2024

1. Introduction

The Dementia Tester App ("we", "our") is designed to help assess cognitive abilities. We take your privacy seriously, especially given the sensitive nature of health-related data.

2. Data We Collect

  • Account Data: Email address and name (via Firebase Authentication).
  • Health & Cognitive Data: Responses to health surveys, results of cognitive assessments, and scores from mini-games.
  • Activity Logs: Timestamps and types of activities performed within the app to track progress.
  • Images: Profile pictures if uploaded by the user.

3. How We Use Your Data

We use the collected data to:

  • Provide personalized cognitive progress tracking.
  • Allow authorized Healthcare Providers (Doctors) to review your assessment results.
  • Authenticate your account and secure your data.

4. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely using Google Firebase. We implement Firebase Security Rules to ensure that only you (and your authorized doctor) can access your specific health records.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your data to third parties. Data is only shared with "Doctor" accounts within the app if you have established a patient-provider relationship through the app's features.

6. Your Rights (Data Deletion)

You have the right to access or delete your data at any time. You can delete your account directly through the app settings, which will remove your personal identifiers from our authentication systems.
